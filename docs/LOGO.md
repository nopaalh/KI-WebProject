Design one sophisticated memorable monochrome black logo mark, NO WORDMARK. Concept: two elegant interlocking open arches/ribbons suggest a patient and doctor meeting, with a small precise medical plus created by negative space at their intersection. Geometric, balanced, restrained, human and modern. Flat crisp vector-like silhouette with smoothly rounded ends, consistent substantial stroke weight, superb legibility at tiny sizes. A unified compact sculptural symbol rather than a set of separate icons. Center the mark with modest clean padding, occupy about 80% of a square canvas. Solid near-black ink only, actual transparent background. No square or rounded-square container, no circular badge, no enclosing border or frame, no background panel, no drop shadow, no gradients, no 3D, no texture, no text or letters, no watermark, no mockup. Avoid a generic medical cross alone; it must be a distinct open intertwined symbol.
